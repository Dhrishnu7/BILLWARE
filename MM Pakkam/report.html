🏷️ Fix missing HSN codes
Set the code once per medicine — it is applied to that medicine's past bills and used automatically on future ones. Amounts, quantities and tax are not touched; this only fills in a missing reporting code.
Most tablets and capsules are 30049099. Type to search, or pick from the list. Leave a row blank to skip it.
⚖️ Correct impossible GST rates
These lines were saved with a GST rate that does not exist, so the return cannot be filed. Pick the correct rate for each.
The bill amount does not change. What the customer paid stays exactly as it is — only the tax split inside that amount is corrected. Your printed bills stay true.
Most medicines are 12%. Life-saving drugs are often 5%, and a few are 0%. Leave a row on "— choose —" to skip it.
🔍 Duplicate purchase entries
Take a Backup first. Deleting these lowers your stock, your purchase totals and the input credit claimed — which is correct, because they were counted twice — but it cannot be undone from this screen.

Selling to a clinic or another business? Their invoices must be filed B2B so they can claim input credit. That happens automatically once you put their GSTIN on their customer record in the Directory — anyone without one is treated as a counter sale, which is right for ordinary customers.

Check it on the portal, not in the offline utility. The offline utility only opens files the portal itself produced, so it cannot read this one. 🧾 Correct these GSTINs
Check each against the customer's own invoice or letterhead. The box turns green when the number passes its check digit. If you cannot get the right one, clear the box — a blank GSTIN files correctly as a counter sale, a wrong one does not file at all.
📅 Correct these bill dates
Under GST the invoice date is the date of supply — the day the goods actually went out. Where the Audit Trail recorded when the bill was saved, that day is offered below; check it against the paper before accepting it. Only the date changes. Amounts, items, tax and the bill number are untouched.
🧾 e-Invoice & e-Way bill
Builds the JSON file the government portal imports in bulk. Upload it at einvoice1.gst.gov.in (or ewaybillgst.gov.in) and the portal returns the IRNs and signed QR codes. Uses the From / To dates on this page.
Do you need this? e-invoicing applies only to B2B sales, and only once your annual turnover crosses the notified limit (₹5 crore). Ordinary counter sales are exempt — they are left out of the file on purpose. An e-way bill is needed when goods are transported above the value below.
